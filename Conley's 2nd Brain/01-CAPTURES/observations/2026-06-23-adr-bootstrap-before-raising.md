Recorded ADR-0003: bootstrap to $1M ARR before opening a raise conversation. The decision is that DailyChew stays bootstrapped through the riskiest phase. Raising early means selling equity at maximum uncertainty and minimum valuation, and importing board/process overhead that works against a solo high-leverage operating model. The product is profitable-by-design at ~13% launch margin growing to 35%+, and the three-year model shows it can reach $1M ARR on one person's time. Revisit trigger: sustained traction above the aggressive scenario, a clear capital-efficient growth channel needing upfront spend, or a compelling inbound offer. Re-examine at each $250K ARR milestone.
